Getting started

Add the widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search WidgetSimple and pick a size.

Choose your apps

Touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget. iOS only lets the system list the apps on your iPhone, so they are picked there. Tapping a word on the widget opens that app.

Change the look

Open WidgetSimple and tap the widget under On your Home Screen: font, colour, size, alignment, letter case, clock and placement apply to all your launcher widgets at once. Templates gives you ready-made looks.

The widget shows a background

Widgets sit on the standard iOS widget background. With the Clear or Tinted Home Screen appearance (Home Screen › Edit › Customize) iOS shows it as glass over your wallpaper.

Subscription

WidgetSimple Pro is a yearly auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. New subscribers may get a free trial; the price and trial length are shown before you confirm.

  • Cancel or change: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › WidgetSimple, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Or in the app: Settings › Manage subscription.
  • New iPhone or reinstall: in the app, tap Restore Purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
