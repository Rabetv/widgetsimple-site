The app

  • Your widget settings (chosen apps, fonts, colours, layout, templates) and fonts you import are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage and in the storage it shares with its widget. They are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we receive no name, e-mail address or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether the WidgetSimple Pro subscription is active on your Apple Account.
  • Crash reports: if you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs.
